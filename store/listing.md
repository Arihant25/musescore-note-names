**Name:** Note Names for MuseScore

**Summary (132 chars max):**
See the name of every note on musescore.com sheet music. Plus a focus mode that hides everything but the score.

**Category:** Education

**Language:** English

**Description:**

See the name of every note on musescore.com. Read a new piece without counting lines.

This extension works like the Note Names plugin in the MuseScore app. You don't need the app. Open any score on musescore.com and every note gets its name.

It reads the score like you do:
• It checks the clef and the key signature.
• Accidentals last until the barline. Ties carry them over.
• Double sharps and double flats work.
• Every note in a chord gets a name.
• Treble, bass, alto and tenor clefs work. So do clef changes.

Choose how names look:
• C D E, German (H, Fis, Es) or Do Re Mi.
• Next to each note, above the staff or below it.
• Pick a size and a colour. You can also colour each letter.
• Show octave numbers if you like.

Focus mode:
One click hides the clutter. Banners, sidebars and pop-ups all go. The pages and the player stay.

Shortcuts:
• Alt+N shows or hides the names.
• Alt+Shift+F turns focus mode on or off.
• Esc leaves focus mode.

Free and open source. No account. Nothing tracked.
Some old scores are only images. Their notes can't be read.

Not affiliated with MuseScore or Muse Group.

## Privacy practices tab

**Single purpose:** Shows note names on musescore.com sheet music and offers a distraction-free view of the score.

**Permission justifications:**
- `storage`: Saves the user's settings (naming system, position, size, colour, focus mode).
- Host permission `musescore.com` and `*.musescore.com`: Reads the score page images (SVG) that musescore.com shows. Later pages come from `s3w.musescore.com`. The notes are read from these images to draw the names.

**Remote code:** No. All code ships in the package.

**Data usage:** The extension collects no user data. Tick none of the data types. Certify all three statements.
