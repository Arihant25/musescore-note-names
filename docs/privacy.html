Note Names for MuseScore

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

The short version

The extension collects no personal data. It sends nothing to the developer. There are no ads, analytics or accounts.

What it does

It only runs on musescore.com. It loads the score images the page is already showing you. It reads the notes from them inside your browser. Then it draws the names on the page.

Focus mode hides parts of the page. It does not change your musescore.com account.

What it stores

Only your settings. Chrome saves them in its own sync storage. They never reach the developer.

Permissions

  • Storage keeps your settings.
  • musescore.com access lets it read the score images.
